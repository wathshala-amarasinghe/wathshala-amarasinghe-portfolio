The challenge was to redesign the agency's portfolio to better reflect their bold, avant-garde style while ensuring the work remained the focal point.

Our goal was to create a digital experience that was as creative as the agency itself, using unconventional layouts and striking typography.

The result is an award-winning portfolio site that perfectly captures the agency's unique brand identity and showcases their work beautifully.
