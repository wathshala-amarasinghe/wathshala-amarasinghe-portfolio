## Overview

Medi Connect is a comprehensive healthcare web platform designed to support hospital operations including patient management, laboratory results, pharmacy billing, and dashboards for healthcare professionals.

## Challenge

Designing for healthcare requires balancing information density with clarity. Clinical staff need fast access to critical data while maintaining zero tolerance for error-prone interfaces.

## Approach

I led the end-to-end UI/UX design from research through to implementation. I applied WCAG accessibility standards throughout, created a modular design system in Figma, and worked collaboratively with backend developers during the handoff and implementation phases.

## Outcome

A fully responsive, accessible web application built with React.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Vite. The platform serves as the official Medi Connect product and supports multi-role access across clinical departments.
